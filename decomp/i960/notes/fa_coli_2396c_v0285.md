# fa_coli poly cluster `0x2396c` — v0285

Native C recovery of the dominant remaining callee inside `0x23524`.
Replaces the six-step `0x23878` bitremap walk in the hybrid parent with
two native `0x2396c` entries.

## What the leaf does

Two PUNCH-driven invocations (`g7` = fighter0 then fighter1):

1. **stq copy** — 30 remapped triples from `src_base[slot][i*12]` plus a
   ROM extra word at `0x020078a8+0xc+index*16` into `g7+0xd00[index]`.
2. **Threshold setup** — `g13+0xfc = +0.05`, `+0x100 = -0.1`,
   `+0x104 = scale_b+0.05`, `+0x108 = (g7+0x1c)+0.05`; zeros four words
   at `+0x11c` and four at `+0x12c`.
3. **30-trip scan** — float `subr`/`addr` on each cluster triple; warm
   always setbits `+0x10c` and `+0x118`, never `+0x110`/`+0x114`.
   Inner 4-trip direction loop never takes the positive path.
4. **Three inlined `0x23878` remaps** — `+0x114&g4` (empty), `+0x10c&~g4`
   (full 30 bits), `+0x118` (full) into `+0x624/+0x614/+0x618`.
5. **4-trip max scan** — `r6` starts at 4; warm counts stay 0 so the
   final pair comes from `+0x12c[4]` and dir table entry 4.

## Fail-closed siblings

  `+0x110`/`+0x114`

- max-count update (`count > 0`)
- `dir_count != 4`
